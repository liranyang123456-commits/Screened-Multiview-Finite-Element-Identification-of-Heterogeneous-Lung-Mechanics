# AIIM manuscript evidence map

## Frozen evidence boundary

This manuscript treats the following files as immutable quantitative evidence:

1. `results/lung_mesh_ai_v2/publication_gates_final.json`
2. `results/lung_mesh_ai_v2/node_response_calibrator_strict.json`
3. `results/lung_mesh_ai_v2/fair_multiview_fem50_strict/fair_multiview_summary.json`
4. `results/lung_mesh_ai_v2/multiseed_node_summary/multiseed_aggregate.json`
5. `results/lung_mesh_ai_v2/response_baseline_benchmark.json`
6. `results/ion_geometry_ood/formal_validation/aggregate.json` and its three
   case JSON records

Material ground truth is synthetic in both mechanics cohorts. The expanded ION
assessment contains 27 de-identified real CT-derived meshes and explicitly
reports no patient material ground truth. It supports geometry-domain numerical
stability and held-out recovery of implanted synthetic mechanics, not
real-patient material accuracy or clinical validity.

The 250 synthetic geometries are generated by
`make_synthetic_ct_surrogate`: a 7×7×3 tetrahedral grid is modified by
procedural curvature, obliquity, and a front-surface sinusoid. They are
CT-like local lung-wall surrogates, **not patient CT meshes and not
CT-conditioned samples**. Real CT-derived geometry appears only in the
27-case ION geometry assessment.

## Claim-to-evidence traceability

### Cohort and protocol

- **250 synthetic patients; 150/50/50 train/validation/test**
  - Evidence: `node_response_calibrator_strict.json`, `protocol.split_counts`.
  - Corroboration: `dataset/sim_lung_ai_v2_multiview250/manifest.json`, `patient_count: 250`.
- **Four experiments per patient**
  - Evidence: all patient records in the screened fair-FEM summary report `experiment_count: 4`.
  - Corroboration: the manifest reports 1,000 experiments for 250 patients.
- **Three views and seven frames**
  - Evidence: manifest `generation_config.num_views: 3`; `dataset/sim_lung_graph.py` requires `sequence_length == 7` and constructs multiview dynamic tensors.
- **Patient-level separation**
  - Evidence: manifest `patient_level_split: true`; the data loader builds one graph per patient and concatenates that patient's experiments.
- **Fixed template discretization**
  - 256 total nodes, 64 observed surface nodes, and 882 tetrahedra for
    template-consistent synthetic samples.
  - Evidence: frozen sample records and `make_tet_grid(7, 7, 3, ...)`.
- **Observation identity**
  - RGB images are not stored in the frozen benchmark. Inputs are rest/deformed
    image-plane tracks, depth change, confidence/visibility, force, and camera
    metadata.

### Leakage-control and estimator identity

- **Model selection**
  - Train-only, fixed five-fold shuffled K-fold, seed 2026.
  - Main selected estimator: standardized PCA plus ridge, 64 components, alpha 1.0.
  - Radius selected estimator: PCA plus ridge, 80 components, alpha 1.0.
  - Evidence: `node_response_calibrator_strict.json`, `protocol`, `selected_main_model`, and `selected_radius_model`.
  - Implementation: `experiments/train_lung_response_calibrator.py`.
- **Uncertainty**
  - Full validation split calibrates finite-sample 90% absolute-residual conformal widths.
  - Test is evaluated only after model and widths are frozen.
  - Implementation: `conformal_quantile`, `conformal_metrics`, and the test policy in the calibrator script.
- **Terminology constraint**
  - The primary result is from a response calibrator operating on image-plane track summaries, not raw images and not the MeshMaterialGNN.
  - The manuscript therefore avoids calling it an end-to-end image model.

### Screened finite-element refinement

- **Learned screened MAP**
  - Background error: `0.04110740128627288` = 4.1107%.
  - Ratio error: `0.18692415951587435` = 18.6924%.
  - Optimizer/screened termination success: `1.0`.
  - Median function evaluations: `7.0`.
  - Median wall time: `24.575721350003732` s.
- **Fixed initialization**
  - Background error: `0.34135420257031346` = 34.1354%.
  - Ratio error: `0.4486981464901718` = 44.8698%.
  - Success: `0.86`.
  - Median function evaluations: `79.0`.
  - Median wall time: `285.8010787499952` s.
- **Evaluation reduction**
  - Exact value: `0.9113924050632911` = 91.1392%.
  - Calculation: `(79 - 7) / 79`.
- **Shared protocol**
  - The fair-run script sets the fixed material start to 5,000 Pa and ratio 1.8 while retaining predicted center/radius.
  - Both arms use the shared held-out patients and frozen multiview FEM settings.
- **Success caveat**
  - In `evaluate_sim_lung_v2.py`, screening rejection is classified as convergence with termination reason `insufficient_initial_physics_evidence`.
  - Therefore, 100% success is an algorithmic termination metric, not a substitute for material accuracy.
  - Strict matched-region termination discordance is 7 learned-only versus
    0 fixed-only cases.
- **Refinement acceptance**
  - Learned screened MAP acceptance is `0.08` (4/50); 46/50 cases retain the
    calibrator estimate after screen rejection.
  - Fixed-initialization acceptance is `0.94`.
  - Interpretation: most final learned-arm accuracy comes from the calibrated
    initializer. FEM primarily audits and screens; it does not routinely
    correct the estimate.
- **Track fitting identity**
  - Learning uses all seven frames.
  - Frozen FEM uses frames 1--5 and envelope regression coefficients
    `(0.45, 1.0, 1.0, 0.65, 0.25)`, followed by two surface-smoothing passes.
  - Confidence is only a mask (`>=0.2`). The residual is not
    confidence-weighted.
  - Predicted-region emphasis is a Gaussian spatial weight with configured
    value 8.0.
- **Implemented MAP data term**
  - Rest and deformed nodes are projected separately and then differenced.
  - Each load/view residual is divided by its RMS track scale, lower bounded by
    the 0.05-pixel noise level.
  - Huber threshold is 1.5; robust losses are summed over load/view
    observations.
  - The material prior is diagonal Gaussian with weight 0.05; log force scale
    has Gaussian prior SD 0.05.
  - FEM uses nodal-to-element modulus averaging, Neo-Hookean mechanics, and
    eight load steps.
  - Pattern-search bounds are 1,000--15,000 Pa, ratio 1--5, and force scale
    0.25--4. Screen and final acceptance thresholds are both 0.05; failed final
    acceptance rolls back to the initializer.
- **Protocol-label caveat**
  - The aggregator's top-level string says `"large held-out-patient noisy 3-D surface motion"`, but the frozen run name/configuration and underlying evaluator use multiview image tracks. The manuscript describes the actual track protocol and does not rely on the stale generic aggregator label.

### MeshGNN representation and loss

- Dynamic token dimension: 14 = flow/depth (3), confidence/visibility (2),
  signed-log force (3), and camera direction/axis (6).
- One seven-frame transformer layer; confidence-weighted temporal pooling;
  view attention adds log-confidence; masked load attention follows.
- Four residual message-passing layers, mean/max global pooling, bounded
  Gaussian global heads, and node log-modulus/SDF/partition heads.
- Frozen loss weights: background NLL/Huber 1/2; ratio NLL/Huber 6/12;
  center NLL/Huber 0.5/3; radius NLL/Huber 0.5/3; heterogeneity 0.5;
  node 0.20; SDF 0.75; partition 1.0; eikonal 0.05; smoothness 0.02.
- Gaussian neural uncertainty is a representation-study output and is not the
  source of the primary split-conformal intervals.

  `results/ion_ct_expanded120_final/benchmark.json`,
  `results/ion_ct_expanded30_physics16/benchmark.json`, and
  `results/ion_ct_expanded_summary.json`.

## Method-to-code map

- Mesh topology and patient graph construction: `dataset/sim_lung_graph.py`
  - Tetrahedral edges become bidirectional graph edges.
  - Static channels are normalized coordinates, fixed mask, and surface mask.
  - Dynamic channels are flow, normalized depth change, confidence, visibility, known force, and camera pose.
- Mesh neural representation: `models/lung_mesh_material_gnn.py`
  - Confidence-masked seven-frame transformer.
  - View and load attention.
  - Four-layer residual message passing in the frozen default configuration.
  - Bounded probabilistic global heads and node material/SDF/partition heads.
- Neural training and metrics: `experiments/train_lung_mesh_gnn.py`
  - Heteroscedastic NLL plus point, classification, node, SDF, partition, eikonal, and smoothness terms.
  - AdamW, cosine schedule, gradient clipping, validation early stopping.
- Strict response calibration: `experiments/train_lung_response_calibrator.py`
  - Train-only candidate selection, separate radius head, validation-only conformal calibration, one-time test evaluation.
- Common-input response baselines:
  `experiments/benchmark_lung_response_baselines.py`.
- FE inverse solver: `lung_inverse_rendering/evaluate_sim_lung_v2.py`
  - Shared patient material across experiments, projected multiview residuals, force/material priors, bounded pattern search, refinement acceptance, and early screening.
- Fair comparison orchestration: `experiments/run_fair_multiview_fem.py`.
- Patient-level bootstrap and paired summaries: `lung_inverse_rendering/aggregate_large_eval.py`.
- Multiseed aggregation: `evaluation/aggregate_multiseed.py`.
- Geometry-only OOD checks: `evaluation/evaluate_lung_geometry_ood.py`.
- CT synthetic-mechanics generation:
  `experiments/generate_ion_ct_synthetic_mechanics.py`.
- CT synthetic-mechanics benchmarking and nested analysis:
  `experiments/benchmark_ion_ct_synthetic_mechanics.py`.
- Privacy-minimized CT figure export:
  `experiments/export_ion_ct_figure_assets.py`.
- Publication gates: `experiments/check_lung_ai_v2_gates.py`.

## Development-validation interpretation

Stage-40, stage-100, stage-250, and summary variants are development records.
Dataset size, features, and architecture changed together. They may motivate
the final design but must not be presented as a controlled scaling law or as
isolating one architectural component.

## Numbers intentionally excluded

No result from `paper_tbme` experiments involving sim_v3, C3VD, ETS, small-bowel force, or unrelated rendering benchmarks is used as evidence in this manuscript. Bibliographic reuse from `paper_tbme/references.bib` does not imply reuse of those experiments.
